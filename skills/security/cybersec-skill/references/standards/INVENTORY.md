**Reading *Playbooks*:** number of Anthropic-Cybersecurity-Skills playbooks that cite the standard in their frontmatter or text. ## Date and status columns

- **Date** is the publication date of the version this skill incorporates (tier A) or of the current version (tiers B and C). For NIST publications cited without a revision, it is the date of that base publication.
- **Status** is one of: *Current* · *Living* (continuously maintained, no fixed version) · *Not current* (withdrawn, superseded or obsoleted) · *Unmaintained* · *Newer release exists* (the version incorporated here is not the latest) · *Not checked*.
- How each status was established: **✓** checked online against the official source named next to it (CSRC publication pages, the RFC Editor index, the project's release metadata); **≈** editorial knowledge recorded in the generator, not verified online — re-check before relying on it in a formal opinion.
- Entries that are not current are listed in their own section, [Not current](#not-current-revisions-and-standards), not in the tier tables.
